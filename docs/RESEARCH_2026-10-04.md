**Findings to remember**
- Querying by city (`timingsByCity?city=Manama&country=Bahrain`) returned the same times but its metadata showed nonsense coordinates (8.89, 7.78). Use explicit coordinates and `timezonestring=Asia/Bahrain` in any build.
- The Bahrain authorities publish official timetables (Sunni: Ministry of Justice / Islamic Affairs; Ja'fari: the Ja'fari Endowments). Calculated times can differ from them by a few minutes, which is why the cards say "Calculated times". Before going live, compare one week with both official tables and add per-prayer `tune=` offsets if needed.

**Terms** (https://aladhan.com/credits-and-terms and the API page, read 2026-10-04): no stated restriction on automated or non-commercial use, no required credit wording, no published caching rule or rate limit. The service is provided "without any warranty" and the page warns that computed times "may not be accurate by some standards". Safe defaults: fetch once per week and cache, show "Calculated times" on every card, and credit "Prayer times: AlAdhan.com (calculated)" as a courtesy.

## 2. Hadith series (Thaqalayn API)

**API**: `https://www.thaqalayn-api.net/api/v2/` (REST + GraphQL, no key). Endpoints used: `allbooks` (39 books), `{bookId}/{id}`, `query/{bookId}?q=`, `query?q=`, `random`. Site: https://thaqalayn.net (each hadith has a `URL` to its page).

**Books** (39): al-Kafi (8 volumes, tr. Muhammad Sarwar), Man La Yahduruh al-Faqih (5), Tahdhib al-Ahkam (4), the books of al-Saduq (al-Amali, al-Khisal, al-Tawhid, Ma'ani al-Akhbar, Thawab al-A'mal, 'Uyun Akhbar al-Rida, Kamal al-Din, Sifat/Fada'il al-Shi'a, Fada'il al-Ashhur), al-Mufid's al-Amali, Nahj al-Balagha, Risalat al-Huquq, Kitab al-Mu'min / al-Zuhd (al-Ahwazi), Kitab al-Ghayba (al-Nu'mani, al-Tusi), Kamil al-Ziyarat, Kitab al-Du'afa', al-Imama wa'l-Tabsira, and Mu'jam al-Ahadith al-Mu'tabara (al-Muhsini).

**Fields per hadith**: `id, bookId, book, category, chapter, author, translator, englishText, arabicText, majlisiGrading, mohseniGrading, behbudiGrading, gradingsFull[] (grade, grader, reference), URL, volume, chapterInCategoryId, thaqalaynSanad, thaqalaynMatn`.

**Grading**: most of al-Kafi carries al-Majlisi's grading from Mir'at al-'Uqul (صحيح sound, حسن good, موثق reliable, حسن كالصحيح good-as-sound, مرسل/مجهول/ضعيف weaker). Some books add al-Muhsini or al-Behbudi. Safe rule for a public series: only صحيح / حسن / موثق (and their combinations), and show the grading and the source on the card.

**Important for design**: `englishText` and `arabicText` contain the full chain of narrators (sanad) before the saying; `thaqalaynMatn` (saying only) is empty for these entries. A series needs the saying separated from the chain (manual curation per post, or careful parsing), and the English translations are older (Sarwar) and sometimes awkward.

**Terms**: the site and API do not show a licence or terms page for the texts (checked the API home page). Translations belong to their translators/publishers. Before publishing, ask Thaqalayn (and, where possible, the translator) for permission, and always link the hadith page.

**Sunni source check (for balance)**: `fawazahmed0/hadith-api` (free JSON on jsDelivr, public-domain repository): Bukhari, Muslim, Abu Dawud, Tirmidhi, Nasa'i, Ibn Majah, Malik, Nawawi's 40, Qudsi, Dehlawi; Arabic + English; per-hadith gradings from named scholars. Example: Tirmidhi 1924 ("The merciful are shown mercy by ar-Rahman...") graded Sahih by Ahmad Shakir and al-Albani, Hasan Sahih by Bashar Awad Ma'ruf. The underlying English translations come from published editions, so the same permission question applies. Note: that hadith contains "Who is above the heavens", one of the phrases you asked to watch for.

**Recommendation**: if a hadith series goes ahead, pair one Sunni and one Shia narration with the same meaning on each card (both graded, both referenced), or alternate weeks, and keep themes to ethics (patience, mercy, gratitude, parents, charity). Get written permission for the translations first.
