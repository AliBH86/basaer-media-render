## Basaer (بصائر) — Privacy Policy

*Last updated: [DATE]*

Basaer is an independent, non-commercial project based in Bahrain. It shares verses of the Holy Quran in Arabic with an English translation on the Instagram account **@basaer.media.bh**. This policy explains what the Basaer Publisher app does with data. In short: **we do not collect personal data about you.**

### What the app does
"Basaer Publisher" is a Meta app used only by the owner of @basaer.media.bh. It uses the Instagram API with Instagram Login to publish posts and short videos to that one account. It does not let anyone else log in, and it does not act on any other Instagram account.

### Data we process
- **Our own account:** the access token for @basaer.media.bh and the account's id and username, used only to publish our own posts and to check that the connection still works. The token is kept in our automation server's encrypted credential store, never in files or messages.
- **Our own content:** the images, videos and captions we publish. The Quran text and translation come from the Quran.com / Quran Foundation API; no personal data is sent to it.
- **Nothing about you:** the app does not read, store or analyse comments, direct messages, followers, likes, or any information about people who view or interact with the account. We do not use cookies, tracking pixels or advertising.

### Service providers
To make and publish the content we use: Meta (Instagram), a DigitalOcean server running our automation software, GitHub (video rendering), Cloudinary (storage of the images and videos we publish), and Telegram (private review messages to the account owner). These services receive only our own content and account details, not data about you. Each has its own privacy policy.

### Sharing and selling
We do not sell, rent or share personal data. We have none to share.

### Data retention
Our published media stays on Instagram and Cloudinary for as long as the post is online. Draft records (the text of a post and its approval status) are kept on our server for our own review history. None of these records contain information about other people.

### Data deletion
Because the app stores no data about you, there is nothing for us to delete. If you believe the app holds any data about you, email **[YOUR CONTACT EMAIL]** with the subject "Data deletion". We will reply within 30 days, delete anything found, and confirm in writing. You can also remove any app's access to your own Instagram account at any time: Instagram → Settings → Apps and websites.

### Children
The account is suitable for a general audience. The app does not knowingly collect any data from anyone, including children.

### Changes
If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
